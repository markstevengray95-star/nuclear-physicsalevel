# AQA A-level Nuclear Physics Learning Lab

Standalone interactive learning app for **AQA A-level Physics 7408, Section 3.8 Nuclear Physics**.

## Main learning route
The built-in lesson sequencer is designed to take a learner from first principles to independent A* exam application:

1. Atoms, nuclei and notation
2. Rutherford scattering
3. Alpha, beta and gamma
4. Inverse-square law, background radiation and Required Practical 12
5. Random decay, activity and decay constant
6. Exponential decay, half-life and logarithmic graphs
7. Nuclear instability and decay equations
8. Nuclear radius, closest approach and density
9. Mass defect and binding energy
10. Binding-energy curve, fusion and fission
11. Chain reactions and thermal reactors
12. Nuclear safety, risk and evidence
13. A* synoptic mastery

## Modes
- **Student mode**: guided self-study, saved completion and mastery, recommended next lesson.
- **Teacher mode**: classroom presentation view, lesson timings, delivery phases, saved teaching notes and lesson status.
- **Simulation Lab**: interactive models with prediction prompts, physics overlays, model limitations, data mode and exam challenges.
- **Formula Coach**: decay, activity, half-life, inverse square, radius, density, mass-energy and binding-energy calculations.
- **Required Practical 12**: virtual inverse-square gamma investigation with background correction, data collection and graphing.
- **Exam Practice**: immediate feedback and AQA-linked questions.

## Running
Open `index.html` locally or use GitHub Pages. The repository is build-free and uses standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Safety and model note
The gamma investigation in the app is virtual. Real work with ionising radiation must only be carried out under appropriate school procedures and supervision. Microscopic scales and reactor diagrams are schematic teaching models rather than literal scale drawings.
